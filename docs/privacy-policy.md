Privacy Policy  
===  

Thank you for using Resume Revise! We value your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use our native Mac resume editor. No account is required, and your information stays on your Mac.

## Information Collection  

Resume Revise stores your resume content and app settings locally on your Mac. The app does not transmit this information to remote servers or third-party services.

### a. **Local Data Storage**  

- Your resume content and application settings are stored locally on your device.
- We do not collect or transmit this information to external servers.

### b. **Log Data**  

We do not collect log data or usage information through the app. Resume editing and data processing take place locally on your device.

## Data Usage  

Resume Revise does not transmit your resume content or app settings, and we do not use or share that information.

## Third-Party Services  

Resume Revise does not send your resume content or app settings to third-party services or advertising platforms.

## Data Security  

Your resume content and settings remain on your device and are protected by your operating system’s security measures. The app does not transmit this information from your Mac.

## User Control  

- You can manage or delete your resume content and settings on your device. The application does not back up this information to the cloud.
- Application data is stored locally on your device; you can remove it from your Mac when it is no longer needed.

## Changes to the Privacy Policy  

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If there are significant changes, we will notify you via the application or our official website. Please check this page periodically to stay informed about our latest privacy policies.  

## Contact Us  

If you have any questions about our privacy policy or need assistance managing your information, please contact us at: [kennyiseeyou@gmail.com](mailto:kennyiseeyou@gmail.com).  

This policy is effective as of **January 22, 2024**.
