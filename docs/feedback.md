Feedback & Support  
===  

Resume Revise is a native Mac app with a visual editor that helps you customize your resume to fit your job-hunting needs and personal style. No account is required, and your information stays on your Mac.

Your feedback and support are invaluable! If you encounter any issues, have questions, or would like to suggest new features, please feel free to reach out. Your input helps us improve Resume Revise and provide a better resume-editing experience.

You can send feedback through GitHub or email **kennyiseeyou@gmail.com**. Thank you for choosing Resume Revise!
